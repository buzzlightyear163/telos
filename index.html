Questions

The rules, plainly.

Can I lock a coin I already launched?

Yes, if it is a SOL-quoted pump.fun coin, the creator wallet signs, and creator fees still pay that wallet. The lock covers fees from that moment on. Coins that already share fees, pay holder rewards or use a stablecoin quote stay where they are.

Can I change the kit later?

No. The lock is permanent, and that is the point. Holders can check the kit on-chain and know the fees will keep doing that one job.

Who runs the crank?

Anyone. The signer pays for the transaction, collects the fees and keeps 5% of that collect. The program decides whether the kit spends.

Can anyone withdraw the vault?

No. The program is the only thing that can spend the vault, and only on the kit's job. Graduate pays the creator only after the coin has graduated.

Is Floor a guaranteed price floor?

No. Floor buys after spot falls under its line. It is not a resting bid, so a sell can trade through before the next crank.

Where does the 15% go?

It buys $TELOS on the market and burns it, on every crank of every TELOS coin.

How TELOS works.

TELOS launches a normal pump.fun coin and locks its creator fees to one job.

Fee route The program Cranking Existing coins Kits What it doesn't do $TELOS

Fee route

  1. The coin launches on pump.fun, with SOL as the quote.
  2. Creator fees are locked on pump.fun. 15% buys and burns $TELOS. The keeper who runs the crank gets 5%. The other 80% is locked to the kit vault.
  3. A crank runs the kit and is paid from that collect. Burn and Floor spend fees buying the coin. Draw pays one weighted holder each hour. Graduate buys the curve until the coin completes, then pays the creator.

The program

The on-chain program creates the vault, locks pump.fun's fee split, and is the only thing that can spend that vault. It checks every spend against the kit and its settings: the spend threshold, the per-crank cap and the slippage limit.

The kit and its settings are set once, at launch or at lock, and can't be changed afterwards.

Cranking

Anyone can crank a locked coin. The signer pays for the transaction, collects the fees and keeps 5% of that collect. The program decides whether the kit spends.

Cranking is permissionless on purpose. A coin keeps working even if its creator walks away.

Existing coins

A SOL coin that is already on pump.fun can be locked if the creator wallet signs, and if creator fees still pay that wallet. The lock only covers fees from then on.

Coins that already share fees, pay holder rewards or use a stable quote stay where they are.

Kits

Burn

Each crank spends the fees sitting above a threshold, up to an optional cap, and burns what it buys. Nothing in the vault can be withdrawn.

Floor

The floor sits a set percent under the highest price the curve has printed, and that line only moves up. When spot is under it, a crank buys with fees, only enough to lift the price back to the line, and burns the tokens.

Draw

Once an hour, the Draw executor picks one wallet, weighted by token balance. The program verifies the hourly limit and the unpaid pot, then sends that pot from the vault to the winner.

Graduate

Until the coin graduates, each crank spends the fees buying the bonding curve and burns the tokens. Once the curve is complete, later fees are paid to the creator.

What it doesn't do

  • Draw picks one eligible wallet, weighted by its balance, and pays it the whole new-fee pot each hour. A wallet's chance is its eligible balance divided by the total eligible balance at payout time. The bonding curve, empty wallets and program-owned accounts are excluded.
  • Draw does not split fees across holders, pay cashback or dust wallets.
  • Floor buys back after spot crosses its line. It is not a resting bid, so it can't stop a sell from trading through.
  • Graduate pays the creator only after the coin has graduated.

$TELOS is the platform token. 15% of every collect, on every TELOS coin, buys $TELOS on the market and burns it.
